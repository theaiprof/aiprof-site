Pilot

Zero-Sum Trading

Students Fund or Divest a company before and after a team pitch, staking ₹1–15 Cr. The wrong side pays the right side. Teaches investment theses, position sizing and belief revision.

Finance · Strategy · Behavioural Finance · UG & PG · 60–75 min per round

How each simulation is built

Theory

Learning outcomes mapped to course topics, and the mechanism the simulation teaches.

Tool

A browser simulation that runs on the teacher's device, offline once loaded. No data leaves it.

Reality

A debrief that links each decision back to what real firms and investors did.

Cases

Case studies with teaching notes are in preparation.

Teachers

Every simulation page carries a printable facilitator guide: timing, roles, scoring rules and debrief questions. Faculty development workshops on running classroom simulations are planned. To ask about one for your institution, write to hey@aiprof.org.in.

About

The AI Prof is an initiative to make experiential business education available to teachers and students who cannot pay for commercial simulations, especially in tier-2, tier-3 and rural institutions. Everything here is free to use and adapt for non-commercial teaching.

Institutions and organisations interested in supporting or partnering: hey@aiprof.org.in.
