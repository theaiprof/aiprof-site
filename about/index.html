About The AI Prof

The AI Prof is an initiative to make experiential business and commerce education freely available to teachers and students who cannot pay for commercial simulations — especially in tier-2, tier-3 and rural institutions.

Quality education should be freely accessible to every curious student, anywhere. Commercial business simulations carry per-seat fees most Indian tier-2/3 and rural institutions cannot pay, and most faculty are not trained to design or run simulations on their own. This site gives teachers ready-to-run simulations with facilitation know-how, and gives students hands-on practice they can do self-paced or in class.

Partner or support

Institutions and organisations interested in supporting this initiative, hosting a faculty workshop, or partnering in another way: hey@aiprof.org.in.

Contribute a simulation

Have a simulation or case that works in your classroom? Share it and we'll package it with a facilitator guide, student brief and rubric, with credit to you. Write to hey@aiprof.org.in.

Privacy

This site has no accounts, forms, cookies or analytics. Where a simulation console stores data (such as scores during a class), it is stored only in your own browser and is never sent to us.
